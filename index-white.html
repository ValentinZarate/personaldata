About Me

I'm a PhD student in Biological Sciences, specializing in animal ecology and conservation. I study the movement ecology of mammals in production landscapes to identify key areas for conservation and coexistence between mammals and producers.

Research Projects

Primates in Plantations

Studying the ecology and behavior of other mammals and birds.
