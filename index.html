Valentín Zárate - Ph.D. Student

About Me

I'm a PhD student in Biological Sciences, specializing in animal ecology and conservation. I study the movement ecology of mammals in production landscapes to identify key areas for conservation and coexistence between mammals and producers.

Research Projects

Primates in Plantations

I am studying movemement and feeding ecology of Black Capuchin Monkyes living in pine plantations of northeastern Argentina. Our goal is to understand the underlying causes of pine trees bark-stripping behavior. We are testing a primate-friendly solution to mitigate the conflict and promote coexistence between primates and foresters.

Other Species

Other research project I have conducted are focused on ecology and behavior of other mammal species and birds, from lesser anteaters to woodpeckers.
