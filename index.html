Valentín Zárate - Ph.D. Candidate

About Me

I'm a PhD candidate in Biological Sciences, specializing in animal ecology and conservation. I study the movement and behavioral ecology of mammals in production landscapes to identify key areas for conservation and coexistence between wildlife and humans. Also, I have studied mammals and birds behavioral ecology in natural, housing (zoos) and urban settings.

Research Projects

Primates in Plantations

I am studying movemement and feeding ecology of Black Capuchin Monkyes living in pine plantations of northeastern Argentina. Our goal is to understand the underlying causes of pine trees bark-stripping behavior. We are testing a primate-friendly solution to mitigate the conflict and promote coexistence between primates and foresters.
